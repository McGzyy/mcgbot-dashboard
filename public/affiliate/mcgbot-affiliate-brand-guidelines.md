# McGBot Affiliate — Brand & Promotion Guidelines

Use this document with your team, editors, or VAs. Approved assets live in your affiliate dashboard under **Resources**.

## Brand assets

- **Logo:** PNG on light backgrounds only (`/brand/mcgbot-logo.png` via dashboard download).
- **Colors:** Primary violet `#6D28D9`, deep violet `#4C1D95`, ink text `#18181B`.
- Do not recolor, stretch, or add effects to the logo.

## Required disclosures

When you share your tracking link, include clear affiliate disclosure where required:

- Short: *"Some links are affiliate links; I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you."*
- Always pair promotional claims with honest context — no guaranteed profits.

## Prohibited promotion

- Guaranteed returns, "risk-free" claims, or fake results.
- Impersonating McGBot staff or claiming official partnership without approval.
- Bidding on McGBot brand keywords in paid ads without written approval.
- Spam, unsolicited bulk DMs, or misleading landing pages.

## Tracking

- Use your default `/r/XXXXX` link or campaign-specific links from the dashboard.
- Signups must come through your link for accurate attribution and commission.

## Commission schedule (loyalty rev-share)

Recurring % is on **net after Stripe fees** (not the member’s gross invoice). Rates are **per referred member** by their payment number:

**Monthly subscribers**

| Their payment # | Your rate |
|-----------------|-----------|
| 1–5 | 15% |
| 6–11 | 20% (unlocks at their 6th payment) |
| 12–36 | 25% (unlocks at their 12th payment) |
| 37+ | No commission |

**Annual subscribers**

| Their payment # | Your rate |
|-----------------|-----------|
| 1st annual | 15% of net + $5 (Basic) or $10 (Pro) first-annual signup bonus |
| 2nd annual | 20% of net |
| 3rd annual | 25% of net |
| 4th+ | No commission |

**Also:** one-time milestone bonuses at **10 / 25 / 50** qualified actives (**$50 / $125 / $250**); holds ~30 days (monthly) or ~90 days (annual) before approval.

Full tables and qualification rules: affiliate dashboard → **Resources** → Commission reference.

This replaces the older flat **20% then 10%** schedule. Existing ledger rows keep the rate that applied when they were recorded.
